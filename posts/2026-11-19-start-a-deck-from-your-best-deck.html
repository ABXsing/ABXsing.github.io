Start a deck from your best deck

Copilot in PowerPoint can now take its look from a deck you attach. It's a better starting point than a blank prompt.

Microsoft's 4 November release notes say that when you attach an existing deck while creating a presentation with Copilot, it applies the attached file's theme and styles to the new deck. It's listed for PowerPoint on Windows and Mac.

A way to use it: pick the one deck your team already agrees looks right, keep it somewhere everyone can find, and attach it every time. The look stays consistent, and people spend their time on what the slides say.

The release notes don't give licence or rollout details, so check it's turned up for you before building a team habit around it.

If you're running AI adoption, small features like this are easy to teach in five minutes, and they tend to be the ones people remember.

Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/release-notes
