Jensen Huang and what his work built

Jensen Huang resigned as NVIDIA's CEO this week. I like him, I admire what he built, and I'm still working out what to make of it.

He co-founded NVIDIA 33 years ago. I find it hard to imagine the company, or a lot of today's AI, without him.

On 8 November he told staff he had been unfaithful to his wife, and NVIDIA's board started an independent investigation. The reports involve a relationship with a woman US officials believe is a Chinese spy. CFO Colette Kress is now interim CEO and is keeping her CFO role.

The reporting I've read doesn't accuse him of a crime, and the investigations are still running, so I'm not going to guess at what they'll find.

I've always believed personal and professional lives should be kept apart. At that level, they rarely stay that way, and this week showed how quickly one can reach the other.

I don't know yet what to make of all of it. What I do know is that his work changed the industry I work in, and that stays true however this chapter ends.
