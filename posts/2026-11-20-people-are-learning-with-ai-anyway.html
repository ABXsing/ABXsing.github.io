People are learning with AI anyway

63% of US workers who use AI at work say it teaches them skills their employer never formally trained them on.

That's from a survey of 1,046 US workers who use AI tools for work, commissioned by the American College of Education, run by Fractl and released on 12 November. 54% say their employer hasn't yet offered structured programs, and 34% say their employer doesn't formally recognise or reward what they learn through AI. 29% use AI to learn so they don't have to admit they don't know something.

Some caveats: the survey was run in February 2026, it's US-only, and ACE is a college that offers training, so it has an interest in the result.

Even so, the pattern is worth noticing. If you're running AI adoption, people may already be learning with AI quietly, and some would rather not ask. That's a reason to make asking easy, and to find out what people have already worked out before designing training from scratch.
