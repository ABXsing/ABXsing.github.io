Uber scales before showing pilot results

Uber is rolling ChatGPT Work out to more than 30,000 employees. The announcement doesn't say how the pilot went.

The rollout was announced on 22 October and grows from an initial pilot of 1,000 people. ChatGPT Work is an agent inside ChatGPT Enterprise, and it connects to Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar and Slack. Uber says the rollout is supported by an internal AI skills library, approved integrations and dedicated employee training.

Uber and OpenAI say they plan to share adoption, use cases and measurable outcomes as the rollout progresses.

If you're running AI adoption, that support list is the part worth borrowing. It's a more useful plan than a licence count. But we can't yet tell which of those pieces made the difference, and I'd love to see those numbers when they arrive, including anything that didn't work.
