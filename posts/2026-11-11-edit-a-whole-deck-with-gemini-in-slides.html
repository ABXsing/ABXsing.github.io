Edit a whole deck with Gemini in Slides

Gemini in Google Slides can now edit a whole deck from one prompt. There's no admin control for it.

Google announced this on 30 October. In the side panel, Gemini can edit multiple slides or an entire presentation at once. Google's examples include "Summarize slides 5-7 into a single slide" and matching the style of another file. The edits can be reviewed or reverted with undo or version history.

It's on an extended rollout that started on 29 October, so it may take longer than 15 days to appear. It covers Business Standard and Plus, Enterprise Standard and Plus, and some Google AI plans. At launch it works in English only.

A way to try it: do it on a copy of the deck and ask for one change at a time, such as tightening the wording on slides 5 to 7. Then check the changes in version history before the deck goes anywhere.

Workspace customers have promotional access to higher limits through at least 1 December, and per-user limits apply after that. If you're running AI adoption, it's worth trying it with a real deck now and seeing what your team would use it for, because the limits will change after that date.
