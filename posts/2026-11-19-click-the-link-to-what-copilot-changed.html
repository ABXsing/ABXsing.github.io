Click the link to what Copilot changed

When Copilot changes your Excel workbook, it now gives you a link straight to what it changed. I'd click it every time.

Microsoft's 4 November release notes say that after a prompt that modifies the workbook, a link in the Copilot chat response takes you directly to the changes. It's listed for Excel on Windows, the web and Mac.

That link is a quick way to review the edits. A changed formula or a moved range is easy to miss in a large sheet, and spreadsheets tend to get reused long after anyone remembers how a number was made.

A habit worth teaching: ask Copilot for one change at a time, follow the link, and check the change before asking for the next one.

If you're running AI adoption, "check what the AI changed" is easy to say in training. A clickable link makes it something people can actually do in the moment.

Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/release-notes
