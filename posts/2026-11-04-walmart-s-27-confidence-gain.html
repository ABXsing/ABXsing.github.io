Walmart's 27% confidence gain

Walmart says associates who took OpenAI training reported a 27% confidence gain. It's an encouraging number, and it leaves the next question open.

CNBC reported on 27 October from its Workforce Executive Council Summit, where Walmart's Maren Waggoner discussed the company's AI training. Walmart offers AI learning and certification programs through Google and OpenAI at no cost to associates. Its June survey on AI attitudes drew more than 1.7 million responses.

The report doesn't give a baseline or comparison group for the 27%, and it doesn't include productivity or performance data.

That doesn't make the number useless. If you're running AI adoption, confidence is a fair early signal, because people who feel lost tend not to try. But it's self-reported, so I'd treat it as the first measure and not the finish line. The harder follow-up is whether people work differently a few months later.
