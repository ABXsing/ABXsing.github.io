AI workflows in Teams, if the app is on

Microsoft Teams now has AI workflow templates in the Workflows app. Whether you see them depends on one admin setting.

Microsoft's 4 November release notes list AI Workflows in the Teams Workflows app, on Windows and Mac. They're off by default unless the Workflows app is enabled. To try one, open the Workflows app and pick an AI workflow template.

My suggestion for a first one: choose a task your team repeats every week and already does the same way each time. That makes it easy to tell whether the workflow got it right.

If you're running AI adoption, check with IT whether the Workflows app is on before you mention this in training. Nothing loses a room faster than a demo of a button nobody can find.

Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/release-notes
