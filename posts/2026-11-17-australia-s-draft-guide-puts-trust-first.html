Australia's draft guide puts trust first

The federal government has released a draft guide on adopting AI at work. The minister says trust with the workforce is the key.

The draft best-practice guide was released on 12 November. AAP reported that it's voluntary and was built on months of roundtable talks with unions and employer groups, with Microsoft, Commonwealth Bank, Kmart and Telstra also consulted. It calls for direct consultation with staff about AI and for careful implementation.

Minister Amanda Rishworth said "What's key to (the guide) is trust with the workforce." She also said it could help with keeping jobs and with upskilling.

It's still a draft, so the details may change.

If you're running AI adoption, consultation is the part I'd act on now. Telling people about an AI tool after it's chosen is different from asking them before. Asking first takes longer, but in my view it's where you find out which tasks people actually want help with.
