Gemini in Gmail reaches every Workspace user

Gemini in Gmail is coming to every Google Workspace user aged 18 and over. It's on by default.

Google announced on 10 November that two features previously limited to consumer accounts and some Workspace editions are now part of the core Workspace experience. One is AI Overview summaries at the top of long email threads. The other is a non-personalised version of Help me write, which drafts or refines an email from a prompt.

The rollout started on 10 November and can take up to 15 days to appear. People need smart features in Gmail turned on. Admins can turn Gemini features in Gmail off, and users can turn them off too.

A small tip for long threads: read the summary, then open the last two or three replies before you answer. The summary tells you what was said, and the latest replies tell you where things landed.

If you're running AI adoption, this is the kind of change that reaches people who never asked for an AI tool. A short note on what it is and when to double-check it will do more than a policy page nobody opens.
