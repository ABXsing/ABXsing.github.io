Check the sources in Fill with Gemini

Fill with Gemini in Google Sheets now shows the web sources behind each cell. That makes a simple habit possible before anyone trusts a filled column.

When the AI function generates cell values, Gemini attaches the web sources it used to each cell. Google has also expanded capacity to up to 3,000 cells per request. The sources are available in Sheets on the web, and the feature is on by default for people with Gemini in Sheets enabled.

The rollout started on 20 October and can take up to 15 days to appear. It covers Business Standard and Plus, Enterprise Standard and Plus, and some Google AI plans and add-ons.

The habit is to spot-check a sample of cells against their sources before the sheet goes anywhere, especially the odd-looking ones.

This matters if you're running AI adoption because bigger requests make a wrong column easier to produce and harder to notice. A small checking step is something people can learn in a few minutes, and it's easier to teach than a general warning to be careful.
